# Phase 5.0 — Human settlement support

TRISOLARIS now bridges environmental conditions to a generic human-population
settlement model.

This is not a medical model and does not predict an individual's health.

## Regional support

For each latitude band the model evaluates broad support from:

- temperature
- oxygen availability
- total pressure
- liquid-water access
- food/agriculture potential

A geometric mean prevents one severe bottleneck from being hidden by strong
scores in other categories.

## Technology

Technology is modeled as a bounded support layer.

It can improve:
- thermal protection
- atmospheric support
- water availability
- food production

It also creates **technology dependency**.

The model therefore distinguishes:

- natural support
- assisted support
- technology dependency

Technology does not cause biological evolution.

## Agriculture

The first agriculture potential combines:
- regional primary-productivity potential
- water availability
- nutrients
- thermal suitability

It does not yet include soils, crops, pests, seasons, storage or logistics.
