# Phase 6.0 — Population lineages and divergence

TRISOLARIS now has the first explicit population-level evolutionary scaffold.

## Rules

Evolution happens to populations across generations.

The model does **not**:
- morph an individual during its lifetime,
- create a species because enough years passed,
- create a third eye because there are three stars,
- infer ethnicity, race or culture from biology,
- turn technology into automatic genetic adaptation.

## Inputs

Lineage divergence depends on:

- generations,
- regional environmental stress,
- isolation,
- gene flow,
- effective population-size proxy,
- technology buffering of environmental selection.

## Traits

The first traits are abstract physiological indices:

- thermal resilience,
- water conservation,
- oxygen efficiency,
- dietary flexibility.

They are intentionally not aesthetic traits.

## Drift and selection

Directional response moves population means toward broad environmental targets.
Genetic drift is represented as a deterministic scenario term that grows with
generations / effective population size and is reduced by gene flow.

This is still a phenotype-level scaffold, not a locus-by-locus genetic model.

## Reproductive compatibility

TRISOLARIS reports a pairwise **compatibility proxy** based on trait distance
and gene flow. It is not a biological test and does not assign species.

Possible classification labels are:

- regional population
- differentiated lineage
- strongly differentiated lineage
- incipient reproductive-isolation candidate

Even the last label is not a species declaration.
