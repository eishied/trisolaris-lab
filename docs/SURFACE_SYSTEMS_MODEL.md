# Phase 3.1 / 4.0 — Atmosphere, water and biosphere potential

TRISOLARIS now connects regional climate to surface conditions.

The model intentionally stops short of inventing life. It calculates whether
surface conditions provide **potential ecological support**.

## Atmospheric layer

Current controls:
- surface pressure
- greenhouse warming already present in the climate experiment

The model derives:
- an approximate pressure-dependent boiling point for water
- a coarse atmospheric-retention proxy for an Earth-mass/radius reference world
- hydrological buffering as a function of pressure

It does **not** yet solve atmospheric chemistry.

## Hydrology

For every latitude band, the model estimates:
- ice dominance
- liquid-water permissibility
- vapor stress
- hydrological-cycle strength

These are proxies. They are not a global ocean circulation model.

## Biosphere potential

TRISOLARIS estimates a potential for primary productivity from:
- temperature
- surface liquid-water support
- hydrological cycling
- an exploratory M-dwarf usable-light factor

This is not evidence of life. It is a boundary-condition model for later
microbial, plant, marine and food-web simulations.

## Why this matters

A globally cold or hot planet can still contain regional refugia. Those refugia
are the locations where future population, ecology, agriculture and evolution
modules should operate.
