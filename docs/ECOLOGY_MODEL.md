# Phase 4.1 — Ecological guilds and food-web support

TRISOLARIS now distinguishes **environmental capacity for life** from **life
actually existing**.

The ecology layer therefore does not invent species. It estimates support for
broad ecological guilds under the current climate, water, pressure, oxygen and
nutrient assumptions.

## Guilds

The first model includes:

- anaerobic microbes
- aerobic microbes
- primary producers
- aquatic primary producers
- decomposers
- primary consumers
- aquatic consumers
- predators
- large aerobic fauna

These are functional guilds, not predicted species.

## Controls

### Oxygen

Oxygen modifies the support potential for increasingly aerobic trophic levels.
The thresholds are ecological proxies and are not claims that a particular
alien organism requires the same oxygen concentration as terrestrial animals.

### Nutrients

A dimensionless nutrient-availability control limits producer and decomposer
potential. Explicit carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus and sulfur cycles come later.

### Seeded biosphere

By default, TRISOLARIS says:

> environmental support only; life not assumed

A user may switch on an experimental seeded biosphere. That changes the
scenario status, not the underlying environmental capacity.

## Food web

Links between guilds use broad trophic-efficiency proxies to communicate energy
flow. They are not population-dynamics equations yet.
