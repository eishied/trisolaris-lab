# Phase 9.2 — Persistent off-world divergence

Phase 9.2 begins only after Phase 9.1 produces an active off-world population.

The model estimates whether a population remains tightly connected to H-01 or
develops a persistent extraplanetary lineage history.

## Causal chain

```
founder cohort
→ colony survival
→ local population history
→ effective population
→ contact / resupply
→ gene flow
→ drift pressure
→ habitat-buffered selection pressure
→ divergence proxy
→ conditional off-world lineage branch
```

## Controlled habitats matter

Destination gravity and settlement burden do not automatically translate into
strong biological selection.

A well-maintained controlled habitat buffers the population from external
conditions. If infrastructure integrity falls, direct environmental exposure
increases and the modeled selection pressure can rise.

## No automatic speciation

The model never creates a species claim.

A persistent off-world lineage means only that ancestry, demography, isolation
and local history have become distinct enough to track as a separate modeled
branch.

No craniofacial, skin, body-shape or other anatomical feature is invented by
this layer.

## Future fidelity

A future SLiM bridge should replace the aggregate drift and selection proxies
with explicit loci, recombination, mutation, migration and selection.
