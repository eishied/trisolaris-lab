# Phase 8.0 — Functional astroanthropology

TRISOLARIS now models how named populations can organize practical responses to
environmental and demographic conditions without assuming a universal ladder
of social or technological progress.

## Core idea

A population does not receive a generic "technology level".

Instead the model tracks a **portfolio of functions**:

- water management
- thermal protection
- open agriculture
- controlled-environment agriculture
- mobility
- infrastructure
- archives / knowledge continuity

Each function can remain strong, weaken, be transferred through contact, or be
rebuilt after demographic disruption.

## Culture

Culture is represented only through functional variables that can be connected
to the simulation:

- continuity of knowledge
- exchange/contact intensity
- differentiation under isolation
- redundancy of food and infrastructure strategies

The model does not infer religion, language, ideology, moral values, ethnicity
or political superiority.

## Population names

The UI assigns stable narrative aliases such as `Aster-1` or `Nival-2` so a
reader can follow populations through the simulation.

These labels are **not inferred ethnonyms** and carry no claim about identity.

## Technology is not a progress ladder

Technical systems are modeled as adaptations with dependencies and failure
modes.

A population can:

- retain a capability
- exchange it with another population
- lose part of it during a bottleneck
- rebuild it later
- depend heavily on one infrastructure path
- maintain redundant alternatives

This makes technological history contingent rather than automatically
progressive.

## Next

Phase 9 connects these persistent populations to multiple planetary
destinations and models:

- launch / transfer feasibility
- founder populations
- off-world demographic bottlenecks
- isolation and gene flow between planets
- new lineages after settlement
- continuity or loss of technology during interplanetary dispersal
