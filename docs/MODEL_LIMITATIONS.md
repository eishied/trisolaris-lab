# Model Limitations

TRISOLARIS LAB must communicate uncertainty rather than hide it.

## High confidence domains
Short-timescale numerical orbital calculations when initial conditions are constrained and numerical error is controlled.

## Moderate confidence domains
Regional climate approximations after validation against established planetary climate models.

## Lower confidence domains
Ecological, demographic and genetic outcomes over long timescales.

## Highly uncertain domains
Specific anatomical forms, cultures, languages, technologies or civilizations millions of years in the future.

## Prohibited scientific shortcuts

The simulator must not assume:
- elapsed time automatically causes speciation,
- three stars cause novel organs,
- environmental stress automatically creates useful mutations,
- technology always progresses,
- biological or cultural traits have a predetermined direction,
- one global mean temperature represents the entire planet.

## Interpretation

TRISOLARIS is intended to explore conditional scenarios and causal mechanisms. It is not an oracle for deterministic deep-time futures.
